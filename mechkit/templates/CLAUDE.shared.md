# CLAUDE.md: small parts

One-off printed parts that do not justify a project of their own. Each part has its own sub-folder with a
`state.md`, `brief.md` and `requirements.json` (created by `mechkit new --where shared`).
Shared tooling and conventions: `{{KIT}}/CLAUDE.md`.

Rules: dimensions are tagged (stated / measured / scaled / assumed) and never guessed; parts are scripted and
parametric; a part that gains bought components, firmware or a second printed part moves to its own project folder
(ask the user: that is gate G0 again).
